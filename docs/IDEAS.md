# Voyager - design notes and roadmap

## Decisions (Sep 2026)

- One building, two looks: the *Departure Point* (`voyager:voyager`) is built either as the
  **Launchpad** or the **End Gate**; both are leveled alternatives of the same entry in the build tool
  (Structurize groups blueprints by the anchor block and labels alternatives with the `name=` tag on
  the anchor). Levels 1-5, level 5 of both is a showpiece.
- Every level of both looks shares one 30x44x28 footprint (levels 1-4 are shifted so the hut block
  sits where level 5's does): the build-tool outline never changes on upgrades. Ground outside the
  design is `structurize:blocksubstitution` (leave the terrain), only the air above is cleared -
  filling dips with solid substitution made the first survival builds crawl.
- Nothing floats on the Launchpad (checked programmatically: every block connected to the ground,
  with and without the rocket; ladders/lanterns/doors supported). The End Gate may float (chorus
  islets, spikes).
- The worker has its own suit model (`client/VoyagerModel`: helmet with open visor, chest panel,
  life-support pack shown while working) registered as MineColonies model type `voyager:voyager`.
  MineColonies resolves citizen textures in its own namespace, so the textures ship as
  `assets/minecolonies/textures/entity/citizen/default/voyager{male,female}1{_a,_b,_d,_w}.png`
  (heads copied from the Nether Miner textures so faces and skin tones stay), icons for all 8 styles.
- MineColonies looks citizen voice lines up by job path (`ModSoundEvents.CITIZEN_SOUND_EVENTS`) and
  crashes the server tick for a job without an entry: every addon job needs one. Voyagers borrow the
  Nether Miner's lines.
- Trip mechanics mirror the Nether Miner's design (supplies in, loot-table roll out, simulated fights
  with adventure tokens, blocks dug with the carried tool) but are our own implementation; the three
  away states reuse `AIWorkerState.NETHER_LEAVE/AWAY/RETURN` because the enum belongs to MineColonies.
  `AbstractEntityAICrafting` only leaves IDLE while `hasWorkToDo()` is true, so the Voyager's version
  also counts being away, counting down or travelling - otherwise a returned Voyager idles forever.
- MineColonies respawns a "travelling" citizen only every 5 minutes or at dawn; `BuildingVoyager`
  calls `updateEntityIfNecessary()` from `onColonyTick` as soon as the travel time is up.
- Launch windows: 1 trip per 3 days (L1-2), 2 days (L3-4), every day (L5); trips per window scale
  with the crew (Buddy System) and Starlight Navigation. Expedition length = finds x 20 s
  (Rapid Refit -25%), then the explore roll and the landing show.
- Research **Reach for the Stars** hangs off *Open the Nether* in the Technology branch; requires a
  Nether Mine, costs 16 ender pearls + 2 eyes of ender; unlocks the Departure Point. Nine follow-ups
  (generated by `tools/gen_research.py`, effect ids in `colony/VoyagerResearch`): Void Insurance I/II,
  Starlight Navigation -> Rapid Refit / Buddy System, Ender Harvest, Shulker Whisperer -> Dragon Hunt,
  Long-range Comms -> Return to Sender. Dragon Hunt: 10% per level-5 trip, an adventure token for the
  ender dragon (12 damage, 250 xp), drops a dragon head + 3-5 dragon's breath in code (she has no
  loot table).
- Gear ladder: iron at level 1, diamond at 2, netherite at 3, anything from level 4
  (`BuildingVoyager.getMaxEquipmentLevel`). Food is requested only while racks + backpack hold
  fewer than 16 rations per crew member (`rationStockWanted`), instead of a full stack of every menu
  item. Before every fight the Voyager eats up to 3 rations (+5 hp each) back to 80% health -
  cumulative damage across a roll's fights (24 -> 4 -> 1 -> dead) was the main cause of deaths.
- The rocket really leaves: every rocket block carries the blueprint tag `rocket`; the building stows
  them (block states in its NBT) at lift-off and puts them back at touchdown. The crew enters through
  a hatch (warped door) at cabin-floor height; the departure point is inside the cabin. While away
  the (invisible) Voyager waits by the hut, not in the cabin - the trench under the rocket is magma.
  If the crew dies the empty rocket lands by itself.
- MineColonies sends a worker to a *walkable cell next to* the work position (same height, never
  the block itself) and only accepts arrival within 7 blocks: the departure point needs a walkable
  neighbour, checked by `walkable_departure()` in `tools/build_pack.py` (a build gate). If the
  Voyager still cannot get there within a minute they say so (interaction) and log a warning.
- Effects live in `fx/Effects` (a server-tick scheduler; tick a *snapshot* of the show list - starting
  a show from inside a show step used to throw ConcurrentModificationException). Particles beyond 32
  blocks need the per-player `sendParticles(force)` (`sprayFar`). The End Gate beam is coloured dust
  (JOML `Vector3f`), full-bright so it shows by day too. Sounds are synthesized by `tools/gen_sounds.py`.
- The portal film glows: panes are tagged `portal`; the building places vanilla `minecraft:light`
  blocks (level 15, checkerboard in front of/behind the panes) itself, takes them down while a work
  order is open (the builder would mine a creative-only block forever) and puts them back afterwards.
- A citizen who dies invisible gets no grave from MineColonies; the AI raises one by the console
  (`GraveManager.createCitizenGrave`) so the Undertaker can bury or resurrect the Voyager.
- Damage type `voyager:the_end` for deaths on expeditions ("was lost in the End").
- Public identity: Lovkar & Claude; everything in English; GPL-3.0-or-later like Colonist Errands.

## Verified in play (0.2.0)

- Both looks, all five levels: placement, survival builds and builder-driven upgrades.
- The full expedition loop on both looks: launch, rocket gone/back, End Gate vortex, flash and beam,
  landing, haul deposited, expedition log written, comms lines, grave for a lost Voyager.
- Buddy System (two Voyagers sharing one Departure Point), the gear ladder, rations.

Not yet exercised: dedicated servers, the Dragon Hunt encounter, Return to Sender, Rapid Refit.

## Ideas for later

- Rare End-city finds at level 5: enchanted books, a "map of the End" lore item.
- A small chance the Voyager brings back a *souvenir* named after the trip (custom item name).
- Config: trips per window, supplies, expedition length, whether deaths are permanent.
- Localizations beyond en_us.
- Colonist Errands: let a player ask a Voyager for the story of the last expedition (2.1.0 adds
  the lore and the crew chatter; a dedicated voice tool could follow).
